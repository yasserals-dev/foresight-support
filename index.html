Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to connect my bank accounts?

No. Foresight works entirely with data you manually enter. We never connect to your bank accounts or access your financial institutions.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell your information to third parties. You can delete your account and all data at any time.

How much does Foresight cost?

Foresight is completely free. All features — including scenario exploration, detailed charts, and full financial history — are available at no cost, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

What devices are supported?

Foresight is available on the App Store for iPhone. Android support is planned for the future.

Is this financial advice?

No. Foresight is a personal planning tool to help you visualize your finances. It's not a substitute for professional financial advice.

How often should I update my data?

We recommend checking in monthly to keep your score accurate. You'll receive optional reminders to help you stay on track.
